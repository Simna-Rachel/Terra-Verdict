📖 HOW TO PLAY
The Premise
You are CEO of a global mega-corporation. Over 8 fiscal years, crises will test your leadership. Every choice you make moves three meters — Profit, Planet and People. Let any one collapse to zero and the world ends.
The Three Meters
💰
Profit — Your financial health. Starts at $600. Spending is sometimes necessary. If you go bankrupt you lose leverage.
🌍
Planet — Ecological health. Starts at 72%. Drop to 0% and civilisation collapses. The hardest meter to recover.
👥
People — Worker and public trust. Starts at 72%. Mass revolt ends the game. High trust unlocks better outcomes.
Each Year
1
Read the crisis. A real-world dilemma hits your boardroom. Understand what's at stake before choosing.
2
Pick a response. Three options with hidden trade-offs. Greed is tempting. Balance is hard.
3
See the impact. Stats update. Dr. Maya Chen gives expert advice. Learn for next time.
4
Policy Blitz (Year 5). A mini-game where you sort policies in 20 seconds. Earn stat bonuses!
5
Survive all 8 years without any meter hitting zero to see your Terra Verdict.
Tips
• Planet below 20% — next bad eco-choice ends the game
• People below 30% — revolt is one choice away
• Balanced play across all 3 meters unlocks the Triple Crown achievement
• Hard mode applies a 1.4× penalty multiplier on negative choices

ℹ ABOUT
The Mission
TERRA VERDICT is a corporate sustainability simulator built to make environmental decision-making visceral and personal. Every choice reflects real trade-offs that corporations face today — between profit, people and planetary survival.
The Science
Each scenario is grounded in real sustainability data. The advisor system — Dr. Maya Chen, Chief Sustainability Advisor — reflects actual ESG research, climate economics and corporate responsibility literature.
